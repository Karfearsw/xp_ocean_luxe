**Important notes**
- **Room rates change constantly** (seasonality, demand, promos). Where possible, I included **“From: $X/night”** from official booking pages, or a published rate range. Treat these as *starting points*, not guaranteed pricing.
- **Policies vary by resort**. Many Westgate pages point to a central Terms & Conditions page for final policy language. Always confirm before publishing anything “hard.”
- **Do not copy images from hotel sites unless you have rights.** Use the “Image sourcing playbook” below.

## D) Image sourcing playbook (what to use for your travel site)

### Best practice (lowest risk)
1. **Shoot your own images** (or pay a local photographer). You own the rights.
2. **Request a media kit / written permission** from each resort brand/management company (especially for Westgate and Rosewood).
3. Use **stock images** for destination-led content (beach, mountains, city skyline) and keep the “property” images minimal unless licensed.

### “Okay but be careful”
- **Link** to official photo galleries (do not download and re-host without permission).  
- Use embeds/links on internal admin pages as “reference,” not as your public CDN.

### Avoid
- Copy/pasting images from Booking.com/Expedia/Tripadvisor/Google without a license.
- Using provider logos in your Ocean Luxe UI (avoid trademark confusion).

### Helpful official gallery entry points (reference links)
- Westgate resorts usually have `/photos/` pages (example: Myrtle Beach includes a “Visit our Photo Gallery” link).  
  https://www.westgateresorts.com/hotels/south-carolina/myrtle-beach/westgate-myrtle-beach-oceanfront-resort/
- Westgate Reservations properties have “Photos” tabs (example: Branson Lakes / Branson Woods).  
  https://www.westgatereservations.com/resorts/westgate-branson-lakes-resort/photos/  
  https://www.westgatereservations.com/resorts/westgate-branson-woods-resort/photos/

## E) Pricing strategy recommendation (how to show rates on Ocean Luxe)

If you’re manually fulfilling via owner inventory, the safest approach is:
- Show **“From $X”** (your computed retail) + “subject to date availability.”
- Make the booking flow **request-based** unless you truly have real-time availability.
- Keep a **seasonal rate table** per resort (Off-peak / Shoulder / Peak) and adjust by unit type.

If you want live pricing:
- Use a booking engine / channel partner API, or create an internal “rate calendar” that ops maintains weekly.
