# Ocean Luxe Estate — Intake Forms, Qualification, and Routing

## Form 1: Trip Snapshot (Step 1 of 2)
Goal: capture enough to route without overwhelming the user.

### Fields
- First name, last name
- Email
- Phone (optional at this step; collect later with SMS consent)
- Travel dates (approx OK)
- Party size + children under 18 (Y/N)
- Primary lane (Family / Couples / Corporate / International)
- Lodging status (booked / considering / need help)
- Top 3 priorities (multi-select)
  - Disney strategy
  - Universal strategy
  - Dining reservations
  - Airport transfers
  - Chauffeur/on-call
  - Celebration/proposal
  - Corporate logistics
  - Shopping
- Budget band (range selector)
- “What would make this trip feel effortless?” (short text)

### Routing rules (example)
- Party size ≥ 5 OR kids = yes → Family pipeline default
- Corporate selected → Corporate pipeline
- Budget band above threshold → “Priority” tag

## Form 2: Concierge Intake (Step 2 of 2)
Goal: produce an itinerary and deliver on the luxury promise.

### Trip details
- Exact dates + arrival/departure flights (if known)
- Hotel/resort + address
- Mobility/accessibility needs
- Stroller/child seat needs (if transport)
- Dining preferences, allergies, must-have restaurants
- Attraction priorities by day (if park-focused)
- Celebration flags: birthday/anniversary/proposal

### Logistics + preferences
- Preferred communication channel (Discord / SMS / email / WhatsApp)
- Concierge support window preference (standard / VIP)
- Transportation preference (SUV / luxury van / executive sedan)
- Luggage count (airport transfers)

### Compliance and consent (placeholders; must be finalized with counsel)
- SMS consent checkbox (TCPA style language)
- Marketing email consent checkbox
- Disclosure acknowledgement if applicable (seller-of-travel / vacation opportunity)

## Westgate “Request Invitation” Form (Invitation-only)
Goal: qualify without creating a “timeshare lead form” vibe.

### Fields (minimal + ethical)
- Are you interested in being considered for resort opportunities that may require attending a presentation? (Y/N)
- Estimated annual vacation frequency (range)
- Preferred travel months
- Household decision-makers: will all decision-makers attend if required? (Y/N/Unsure)
- Best contact channel
- Consent + disclosures acknowledgement (required)

### Routing
- If “No” → tag “Not interested” and keep client in normal concierge nurture
- If “Yes” + threshold met → tag “Westgate candidate” → schedule education call

## Scoring (example)
Score 0–100:
- Dates within 45 days (+20)
- Budget above threshold (+15)
- Party size complexity (+10)
- Transport needs (+10)
- Lodging premium tier (+10)
- “Pain score” high (+10)
- Corporate SLA needs (+15)
- Westgate interest (+10) (only if consented)
